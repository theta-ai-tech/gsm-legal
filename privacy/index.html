GameSetMatch ("GSM", "the app", "we") is a social sports matchmaking app for racket sports — padel, tennis, and pickleball. It helps you find opponents at your level, schedule matches, record scores, and play in leagues.

The app is operated by Ignatios Charalampidis (the "data controller" under the EU General Data Protection Regulation). For anything in this policy, contact iggy.thetatech@gmail.com.

Why we collect it (and the legal basis)

  • To run the service — matching you with opponents, scheduling matches, keeping scores and standings. Basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To fix and improve the app — crash reporting and usage analytics. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in providing a working, improving product.
  • To notify you — match offers, confirmations, and reminders. Basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); you can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • To meet legal obligations where applicable. Basis: Art. 6(1)(c).

What other players see

GSM is a social matchmaking app, so parts of your profile are visible to other signed-in players by design: the name you set, sports, skill level and rating, area, and your match history, results, and league standings. Your email address and sign-in method are never shown to other players. The list of players shown on the Play tab is governed by a setting you control — see Who can see you below.

Who can see you

This section is about one thing: the list of players shown to others on the Play tab.

In Profile, the setting "Players near your level can see you" is on by default when you sign up. You can turn it off at any time in Profile.

When it is on, your entry in that list is exactly four things: your first name, level, area, and usual times — nothing else. No surname, phone number, photo, or precise location is shown. Appearing under a sport discloses that you play it.

The list is not limited to players near your level. It is shown to every signed-in GSM player in your city who plays that sport. When few players are close to your level, the list widens to include everyone in your city for that sport.

Turning the setting off stops you appearing in that list. The list is built only from players who have it on, worked out fresh each time it is shown, with no stored copy — a screen that is already open picks up the change the next time it loads.

This setting governs only that list. When you post an availability slot, send a match offer, or agree a match, your first name, level, and area are published to every signed-in GSM player in your city who plays that sport — whether or not this setting is on.

Being findable by other players is part of the matchmaking service you sign up for, so we process this data on the basis of performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). This is set out on the last page of onboarding when you sign up, and the setting is in Profile at any time.

Who processes your data

We do not sell your data, we show no advertising, and we do not track you across other companies's apps or websites. Data is processed by these providers, acting on our instructions:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), backend hosting (Cloud Run), analytics, and crash reporting. App data is stored in the EU (europe-west8, Milan). Some Firebase services (Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics) may process data on servers in the United States; transfers are covered by Google's Data Processing Terms, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and push notification delivery, if you use them.
  • Google Places — venue search queries, to return court results.

How long we keep it

Account and profile data are kept while your account exists. Match results feed other players' ratings and league standings, so after account deletion, results may be retained in a form no longer linked to you. Crash and analytics data are retained on Google's standard schedules (roughly 90 days for crash data and up to 14 months for analytics) and then deleted or aggregated.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Delete account). This removes your profile and personal data as described above. You can also email iggy.thetatech@gmail.com and we will do it for you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data; ask for a copy in a portable format; object to processing based on legitimate interest; and withdraw consent at any time. Write to iggy.thetatech@gmail.com and we will respond within a month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority — in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Children

GSM is not directed at children. You must be at least 16 to use the app.

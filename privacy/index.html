GameSetMatch ("GSM", "the app", "we") is a social sports matchmaking app for racket sports — padel, tennis, and pickleball. It helps you find opponents at your level, schedule matches, record scores, and play in leagues.

The app is operated by Ignatios Charalampidis (the "data controller" under the EU General Data Protection Regulation). For anything in this policy, contact iggy.thetatech@gmail.com.

What other players see

GSM is a social matchmaking app, so parts of your profile are visible to other signed-in players by design. What is shown depends on where:

  • The players list — your first name, level, area and usual times, only while Players near your level can see you is on (see Who can see you).
  • A post that you want to play — your first name, your level, the sport, the time you want to play, your court if you have one or your area if you need one, and your partner's first name for doubles. A post is visible to every signed-in GSM player, in any city, whether or not you are on the players list; withdrawing the post removes it.
  • Asks and matches — the players you ask or play with or against see the name you set and your level, and the result once the match is scored.
  • Leagues — if you join a league, its standings and fixtures show your name, results and position to other players.
  • Search by name — a player looking for a doubles partner for a league can find you by typing the start of your name, whatever your settings, and sees your name and your rating points for that sport.

Your email address, sign-in method, date of birth and match radius are never shown to other players.

Who can see you

The Play tab has a list of players, starting with those near your level, so that players can ask each other to play. Whether you are on that list is controlled by one setting in the app, Players near your level can see you. It is on by default when you sign up, and you can turn it off at any time in Profile.

When it is on, your entry in that list is exactly four things: your first name (the first word of the name on your profile), your level, your area and your usual times — the last two once you have added them to your profile — and nothing else. It is shown to other signed-in GSM players in your city who play the same sport, so they can ask you to play. No surname, phone number, photo, location from your device, distance or rating points are shown. You appear under each sport you play, so the list also shows that you play it.

The list is shown to every signed-in player in your city who plays that sport, not only to players near your level: when few players are close to your level, the app shows everyone in your city for that sport.

We do this because being findable by other players is the matchmaking service you sign up for. The legal basis is performance of that contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). The last page of sign-up tells you that players in your city will see you and lets you change the setting before you finish; after that, the setting is in Profile at any time.

Turning the setting off removes you from the list immediately: the list is built afresh each time it loads and from that moment no longer includes you.

This setting covers the list only. Other ways players can see you — your own posts, asks and matches, leagues, and search by name — are described above in What other players see, and they do not depend on this setting.

Who processes your data

We do not sell your data, we show no advertising, and we do not track you across other companies's apps or websites. Data is processed by these providers, acting on our instructions:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), backend hosting (Cloud Run), analytics, and crash reporting. App data is stored in the EU (europe-west8, Milan). Some Firebase services (Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics) may process data on servers in the United States; transfers are covered by Google's Data Processing Terms, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and push notification delivery, if you use them, and, if you allow location access during sign-up, turning your device's location into a city name.
  • Google Places — venue search queries, to return court results.

How long we keep it

Account and profile data are kept while your account exists. Match results feed other players' ratings and league standings, so after account deletion, results may be retained in a form no longer linked to you. Crash and analytics data are retained on Google's standard schedules (roughly 90 days for crash data and up to 14 months for analytics) and then deleted or aggregated.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Delete account). This removes your profile and personal data as described above. You can also email iggy.thetatech@gmail.com and we will do it for you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data; ask for a copy in a portable format; object to processing based on legitimate interest; and withdraw consent at any time. Write to iggy.thetatech@gmail.com and we will respond within a month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority — in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Children

GSM is not directed at children. You must be at least 16 to use the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and note the new effective date above, and we will tell account holders about significant changes by email or in the app. The current version is always linked from the app's sign-in screen.
